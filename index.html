About Tarmals Agro

Built for the next stage of agriculture.

Tarmals Agro Limited is a seed processing business based in Arusha, Tanzania. As we prepare to begin commercial operations, our focus is on providing professional contract processing services to seed companies and agricultural businesses.

Our process

From Field to Planting-Ready Seed

A systematic approach to maize seed processing, from receiving raw seed to final packaging.

  1. 01

    Receiving & Moisture Testing

    Assess incoming seed condition and moisture content.

  2. 02

    Pre-cleaning

    Remove larger impurities and foreign material.

  3. 03

    Fine Cleaning

    Remove smaller impurities and unwanted material.

  4. 04

    Grading & Gravity Separation

    Separate seeds according to size and density.

  5. 05

    Seed Polishing

    Remove surface dust and improve seed finish, where required.

  6. 06

    Seed Treatment & Coating

    Apply specified protective treatments and coatings.

  7. 07

    Surface Drying

    Dry the treated seed surface before packaging.

  8. 08

    Weighing & Packaging

    Accurately weigh and pack processed seed.
